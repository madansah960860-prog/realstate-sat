Cookie Policy

What this site stores in your browser, why, and how to change it. Last updated 01/05/2026.

Template notice

This policy is a starting template. It must be reviewed by a real estate attorney licensed in each state where we operate, together with privacy counsel, and the cookie inventory must be verified against what the deployed site actually sets, before it is relied on.

The short version

This site sets no non-essential cookie or similar technology before you choose. Two items are stored regardless, because the site cannot function without them: your cookie choice, and your saved-property list. Neither is used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Everything else, including analytics and advertising, is off until you turn it on, and rejecting all optional storage leaves the entire site fully usable.

Changing your preferences

Use the manage cookie preferences control, which is available here and in the footer of every page on this site. It reopens the consent panel with your current choices, and a new choice takes effect immediately.

You can also clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. Doing so will clear your saved-property list and your recorded consent choice, and the consent banner will appear again on your next visit.

Third-party vendors, including Google

If you consent to advertising cookies, Google and similar vendors may use cookies and comparable technologies to serve and measure advertising on this site and elsewhere. Their use of that data is governed by their own policies.

  • Google Ads Settings, to control personalised advertising from Google: adssettings.google.com
  • Google Privacy and Terms: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out: optout.networkadvertising.org
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out: optout.aboutads.info

Advertising campaigns for this site are self-identified as Housing ads with Google. That designation restricts targeting on the basis of age, gender, parental status, marital status and ZIP code, and limits audience targeting, in support of fair housing obligations. See the Fair Housing Notice.

Third-party content on this site

Photographs on this site are served from a third-party image host, and the web fonts are served from Google Fonts. Requests to those hosts reveal your IP address to them in the ordinary course of loading the page. They are used to deliver the page rather than to track you, and they are not part of the optional advertising category.

Self-hosting the fonts and images at launch would remove these third-party requests entirely, and we recommend it.

What happens if you reject optional cookies

Nothing on this site stops working. Every guide, every community page, every property page, the filters, the gallery, the saved-property list, the estimator and every form continue to function exactly as they do with cookies accepted. You will not be asked repeatedly, and you will not see a reduced version of the site.
