About this residence

A twelfth-floor corner residence in the middle building, with a wrap balcony carrying west and north exposure. Plan D is the second-largest floor plan at Azure Key and the only one with a balcony on two elevations, which matters more than it sounds: the west side is unusable on a summer afternoon and the north side stays comfortable.

The layout is entirely single level with zero-step entry from the elevator lobby. Three bedrooms, each with an ensuite bathroom. The primary bedroom opens directly onto the west balcony and has a curbless shower with a 36-inch entry. All primary circulation doorways are 36 inches. There is no interior step anywhere in the residence.

The kitchen was rebuilt in 2020 with full-height cabinetry, an induction cooktop and a vented hood taken through the exterior wall, which is unusual in this building and required association approval. Flooring throughout is large-format porcelain laid over the original substrate. The laundry is a full room rather than a closet.

Glazing is impact-rated and original to the 2008 construction. Impact glass has a service life and the sealed units on the west elevation show some seal failure in two panes, which is disclosed. Budget for replacement of those units.

What we would flag beyond the glazing: the air conditioning air handler is a 2014 unit and is at the end of its expected life in this climate. The association completed its milestone structural inspection in 2024 with remediation items identified on the parking structure, funded from reserves rather than by special assessment. Ask for the current remediation status letter. And the twelfth floor in this building is served by three elevators, one of which is scheduled for modernisation.

The neighbourhood

Azure Key occupies a 6.2-acre parcel on the Gulf side of the island, set back roughly 210 feet from the mean high water line behind a maintained dune. Tower II is the middle of the three buildings, which means shorter walks to the pool deck and the boardwalk than either end building.

From the lobby: the pool and spa 90 feet, the fitness room 140 feet, the beach boardwalk 320 feet, the kayak racks 260 feet. Outside the community: the nearest restaurant cluster is 0.5 miles, a full grocery 1.4 miles, a pharmacy 1.2 miles, public beach access with parking 0.2 miles, the post office 2.1 miles, a hardware store 3.9 miles, and the nearest hospital with a twenty-four hour emergency department 5.1 miles on the mainland across a bascule bridge.

The island has a continuous sidewalk along the main road and a marked bicycle lane. A seasonal trolley runs the island road between November and April with a stop 0.3 miles from the lobby, and a year-round bus route runs roughly hourly on weekdays.

Azure Key is not an age-restricted community and makes no age-based occupancy claim. Its rules govern the property and its use, not who lives there.

Costs to plan for beyond the price

Condominium fee of $1,890 per month, which covers the master insurance programme, building maintenance, reserves, water, sewer, cable and internet. County property tax, reassessed on transfer. A unit-owner insurance policy covering interior finishes, contents, liability and loss assessment, which is separate from the master policy. A separate flood policy for the interior, because the association's flood coverage stops at the building. Electricity, which on a west-facing twelfth floor in August is a real number. And a home-watch arrangement for the months you are away, which the association requires in the form of a local emergency contact on file.

Loss assessment coverage deserves a specific mention. If the association suffers a loss that exceeds its policy limits, unit owners can be assessed for the shortfall. Ask your insurer for a loss assessment limit that reflects the size of this building, not a default figure.
