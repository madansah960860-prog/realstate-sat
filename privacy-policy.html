Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and retain personal information, and the choices you have. Last updated 01/05/2026.

Template notice

This policy is a starting template. It must be reviewed by a real estate attorney licensed in each state where we operate, together with privacy counsel, and adapted to how the business actually processes data, before it is relied on.

1. Who we are

Meridian Shore Realty, LLC is a licensed real estate brokerage with its office at 1420 Bayshore Boulevard, Suite 300, Sarasota, Florida 34236, United States. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Contact us about privacy by email at privacy@meridianshorerealty.com, by telephone at +1 (941) 555-0148, or by post at the address above marked for the attention of the Privacy Contact.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Inquiry and consultation forms. Name, email address, telephone number, the state or community you are interested in, your timeline, an approximate budget range if you choose to give one, the interests you tick, and the content of your message.
  • Property inquiry forms. The same categories, plus the property you are asking about.
  • Property alerts and email subscriptions. Your email address and the preferences you set.
  • Correspondence. Emails, letters, text messages and call notes, including recordings where a call is recorded and you have been told so at the time.
  • Transaction information. If you become a client, the information necessary to represent you, which may include identification documents, financial information provided for pre-qualification purposes, and transaction documents.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and connection data. IP address, browser type and version, operating system, screen size, referring page and the pages you view on this site, with timestamps.
  • Local storage. Your cookie consent choice and your saved-property list are stored in your browser. The saved-property list stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Analytics and advertising identifiers, but only if you consent to optional cookies. See section 6.

Information from third parties

  • Public records, including county assessor, appraisal district, recorder and permitting records.
  • Multiple listing services, where we hold a data licence.
  • Partner brokerages, where you have been referred to us or by us.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf, such as an email delivery platform reporting a bounce.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To provide brokerage representation and the fee-based services you engage.
  • To send market reports, guides and property alerts where you have asked for them.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, licensing and record-keeping obligations, including those imposed by state real estate regulators.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, including wire fraud attempts against clients.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising, where you have consented to advertising cookies.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described here.

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract: our client relationship system, email delivery platform, website host, form processor, telephone system, e-signature provider and document storage. They may use the data only to provide the service to us.
  • Transaction parties, where you are a client and the transaction requires it: the other side's brokerage, title and settlement agents, lenders you have chosen, inspectors, insurers and associations.
  • Partner brokerages, where you have asked for a referral to a market where we are not licensed. We tell you before we make the introduction.
  • Advertising and analytics vendors, including Google, where you have consented to optional cookies. See section 6.
  • Professional advisers such as our attorneys and accountants, under duties of confidentiality.
  • Regulators and law enforcement, where we are legally required to disclose, or where disclosure is necessary to establish or defend legal claims.
  • A successor entity, if the business is sold or merged. We would notify you.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share it with third-party lead-generation networks. Depending on your cookie choices, some advertising cookie activity may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under certain state privacy laws. Section 9 explains how to opt out.

5. Legal bases and retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it.

  • General inquiries and consultation requests: 24 months from the last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Email subscription records: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record kept indefinitely so we do not email you again.
  • Consent records for telephone and text contact: for the period required to evidence consent, currently at least five years.
  • Transaction and brokerage records: for the period required by the real estate regulator in the relevant state, which is commonly five years or longer, and by tax law.
  • Website server logs: 90 days.

6. Cookies, tracking and Google

We set no non-essential cookie or similar technology before you have made a choice in the cookie banner.

Strictly necessary storage

Two items are stored in your browser regardless of your choice, because the site cannot work without them: your cookie consent record, and your saved-property list. Neither is used for advertising or cross-site tracking, and the saved-property list never leaves your device.

Analytics cookies, optional

If you consent, analytics cookies help us understand which guides are read and where pages fail. This may include Google Analytics.

Advertising cookies, optional, and Google's use of them

If you consent, advertising vendors including Google may set cookies to measure and report on our advertising. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads on this and other sites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google through Google's own Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can opt out of participating vendors' interest-based advertising through the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org and the Digital Advertising Alliance at optout.aboutads.info.
  • You can change your choice on this site at any time through the manage cookie preferences link in the footer of every page.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site remains fully usable if you do.

Advertising campaigns for this site are self-identified as Housing ads with Google, which restricts targeting on the basis of age, gender, parental status, marital status and ZIP code, and limits audience targeting. See the Fair Housing Notice.

Do Not Track

Browsers send a Do Not Track signal inconsistently and there is no accepted standard for responding to it, so we do not respond to it. We do honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of sale and sharing where applicable law requires it.

Full detail is in the Cookie Policy.

7. Lead forms, telephone and text contact

Every form on this site states, beside the submit button, what happens to the information, who will contact you and how to opt out.

Telephone and text follow-up happens only where you have given express written consent by ticking the relevant box. That consent is not a condition of any purchase or service, and you will receive a reply to your inquiry whether or not you tick it. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages, or HELP for help. You can also ask us to stop calling by emailing the privacy address or telling us on any call.

Marketing emails identify us as the sender, carry our physical mailing address, and include a working unsubscribe link that takes effect promptly. Transactional messages relating to a live transaction are not marketing and continue while the transaction is open.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults researching real estate and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 through the forms on this site.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email privacy@meridianshorerealty.com and we will delete it.

9. California privacy rights, and Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to exceptions including our legal record-keeping obligations as a licensed brokerage.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that trigger this right.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights. We do not offer financial incentives for personal information.

How to exercise your rights

Email privacy@meridianshorerealty.com with the subject line "Privacy Request", call +1 (941) 555-0148, or write to the Privacy Contact at our office address. We will verify your identity before acting, usually by confirming information we already hold. An authorised agent may submit a request with written permission.

We respond within 45 days and may extend by a further 45 days where necessary, telling you if we do.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. If you consent to advertising cookies, some of that activity may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law.

To opt out of any such sharing, use the manage cookie preferences control, which appears in the footer of every page on this site, and switch advertising cookies off. You may also email the privacy address with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share". We honour Global Privacy Control browser signals as an opt-out request where applicable law requires it.

Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and others, have broadly similar rights. Use the same contact routes and tell us which state you are in.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit, access controls, multi-factor authentication on business systems and staff training. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

A specific warning. Real estate closings are a frequent target of wire fraud. We will never send you wire instructions by email without also confirming them by telephone, and we will never change wire instructions by email. If you receive wire instructions purporting to come from us, telephone us on a number you already hold, not one contained in the message, before sending any funds.

11. Links to other sites

This site links to third-party sites including government and regulatory resources. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices. Read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices or the law change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Where a change is material we will give notice on the site and, for subscribers, by email.
