Schedule a consultation

Forty-five minutes with a licensed broker, by phone or video. No charge, no obligation, and no follow-up sequence unless you ask for one.

What we will ask you

  • Which state or states you are considering, and why those.
  • How many months a year the property would be occupied.
  • What airport access you need, and to where.
  • What monthly carrying cost is genuinely acceptable.
  • Whether you need to sell something first.
  • Your timeline, honestly stated. Two years is a normal answer.

What you will get

  • A direct answer on whether we are the right brokerage for what you are trying to do.
  • If we are not, a specific suggestion of who is, with any referral fee disclosed.
  • A written summary by email within two business days, including anything we promised to look up.

We will not send you a pre-approval funnel, add you to a drip campaign, or call you repeatedly. If you want us to stay in touch, tell us how often.
