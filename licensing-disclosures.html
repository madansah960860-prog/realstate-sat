Licensing and brokerage disclosures

3. Agency and representation

Agency terminology and the required disclosure documents differ substantially between states, and the summary below is general. In each transaction you will receive the disclosure form that state law requires, at the time state law requires it.

  • Buyer representation. We act for you, owe you the duties the relevant state imposes on a broker representing a buyer, and are paid the fee agreed in your written representation agreement.
  • Seller representation. We act for you under a written listing agreement and are paid from your proceeds at closing.
  • Both sides of one transaction. Where the law of the relevant state permits it, and only with the informed written consent of both parties, we may act in a limited or transaction-broker capacity. In that situation there are things we cannot do for either side, and we will explain exactly what those are before anyone signs. If you would rather not proceed on that basis, we will arrange separate representation.
  • No representation. If you contact us about a property we have listed and you are not our client, we do not represent you. We will still be honest with you, give you accurate information about the property, and tell you that you may wish to obtain your own representation.

4. How commission works and who pays it

Commission is negotiable. It is not set by law, by any association, or by custom, and there is no standard rate.

On the buy side, we agree our fee with you in writing before touring a property. The agreement states the amount or percentage, the term, the scope, how it can be ended and any protection period. Who pays it is separately negotiable and may be the seller, the listing brokerage, you directly, a seller contribution to your closing costs, or a combination. If an offer of compensation is less than the fee you agreed with us, the difference is payable by you. If it is more, we do not retain the excess above the agreed fee.

On the sell side, our fee is set in the listing agreement and paid from your proceeds at closing. Whether to offer compensation to a brokerage representing a buyer, and how much, is your decision, it is negotiable, and it is not required.

5. Referral fees from partner brokerages

Where you want to buy or sell in a market in which we are not licensed, we introduce you to a brokerage that is. We receive a referral fee from that brokerage, paid out of its commission on the transaction.

  • The fee does not increase what you pay.
  • We disclose the arrangement and the percentage in writing before you speak to the receiving agent.
  • You are free to choose a different brokerage, and we will not withdraw help if you do.
  • We do not receive a referral fee for introducing you to a lender, home inspector, insurance agent, attorney or accountant, and we do not accept one.

6. Fee-based concierge services

Relocation and downsizing coordination is a separate, optional service billed as a flat fee, with prices published on the relocation services page rather than quoted case by case. It is not contingent on a transaction, it is not bundled into commission, and declining it does not affect representation.

Third-party costs such as movers, storage and contractors are paid by you directly to those vendors. We do not mark them up and we do not take a percentage.

7. Paid community placements, which are advertising

Developers and homeowner associations may pay us to feature a community on this website. This is advertising and is labelled as such.

  • Every paid placement carries a visible advertising label on the community card, at the top of the community page, and anywhere else it appears.
  • Payment buys placement only. It does not change the research on the page, it does not suppress unfavourable information, and it does not affect the order in which properties appear in search results on this site.
  • We have declined placements for communities we could not write about honestly, and we will continue to.
  • A paid placement is not a recommendation and should not be read as one.

Currently paid placements: Verano Ridge, paid for by the developer. This list must be kept current, and the paying party named, before launch and thereafter.

8. Partner referral relationships

We maintain a list of moving companies, storage operators, estate-sale companies and home-watch services in the markets we cover. Some of these companies pay Meridian Shore Realty a referral fee.

Where that is the case, we disclose it at the point of the recommendation, in the same conversation, and we will tell you the amount or percentage if you ask. Where we obtain competing quotes, the disclosure appears on the comparison sheet next to the vendor's name. You are never obliged to use anyone we recommend.

9. Free market reports and guides

The market reports and guides on this site are free, carry no obligation, and are not behind an email gate. They exist because they generate consultation enquiries, which is how this brokerage acquires clients. That is the commercial purpose and we state it plainly rather than presenting the material as disinterested public service.

10. Listing data and IDX

Every listing, price, address, photograph and MLS number currently shown on this site is illustrative placeholder content, marked as such in the page source, pending connection to a compliant IDX or MLS feed under a signed data licence.

Once live, listing data will be supplied under the rules of the relevant multiple listing service, which govern what may be displayed, the attribution and broker-reciprocity notices required, the treatment of listings whose sellers have opted out of internet display, and how frequently the data must refresh. Listing information is deemed reliable but is not guaranteed and should be independently verified.

11. Advertising claims

We do not make claims we cannot substantiate. You will not find this brokerage described on this site as the leading, top or number one anything, and you will not find any promise about future property values, guaranteed appreciation, risk-free purchases or particular tax outcomes. We do not publish fabricated urgency, invented scarcity, countdown timers or fake reviews.

If you find a claim on this site you believe cannot be substantiated, tell us and we will either substantiate it or remove it.

12. Complaints

To us

Contact the managing broker at legal@meridianshorerealty.com or by post to the office address, marked for the attention of the Managing Broker. We respond in writing.

To a regulator

You may complain to the real estate regulator in the state where the conduct occurred, whether or not you have raised it with us first. The current contact details, addresses and complaint procedures for the Florida Real Estate Commission, the Texas Real Estate Commission and the Arizona Department of Real Estate must be confirmed and listed here before launch.
