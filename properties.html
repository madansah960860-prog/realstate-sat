    How this list is ordered, and what it is not

    Listings on this page are ordered by community and then by internal reference number. No brokerage, developer or seller can pay to move a property higher in this list. Paid community placements are labelled as advertising on the community cards and on the community pages themselves, and they do not affect listing order here.

    This page is not a multiple listing service and it is not a complete picture of any market. It is a small illustrative set. When we work with you as a buyer client we search the full MLS in the relevant market, along with pocket inventory and off-market opportunities that never appear on a public site.

    Nothing here is an offer. Property information is deemed reliable but is not guaranteed and must be independently verified. Square footage, lot size, year built, fees and tax figures come from public records and seller disclosures and are frequently wrong in small ways and occasionally wrong in large ones. Measure anything you are relying on.

    Looking for something not on this list?

    Tell us the constraints that actually matter: budget, single-level or not, dock or no dock, fee tolerance, how many months a year the house will sit empty, and how far you are willing to be from an airport.

    We will come back with a written shortlist, including communities we do not have a page for, and we will tell you where our own coverage runs out.
