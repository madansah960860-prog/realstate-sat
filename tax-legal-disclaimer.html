Tax and Legal Disclaimer

What the informational content on this site is, what it is not, and where the line sits between a brokerage and a professional adviser. Last updated 01/05/2026.

The disclaimer, stated first

Nothing on this website is tax, legal, accounting, insurance, mortgage or investment advice. Meridian Shore Realty, LLC is a licensed real estate brokerage. We are not accountants, tax attorneys, financial advisers, insurance agents or mortgage lenders, and we do not hold ourselves out as any of those.

Tax, residency and domicile outcomes are fact-specific, they vary substantially between states, and the law changes. We make no promise of any tax saving, any assessment outcome, any insurance premium, any financing approval or any investment result.

Before acting on anything you read here, consult a licensed CPA, a tax attorney, a financial adviser, a licensed insurance agent or a licensed mortgage lender, as appropriate to the question.

Template notice

This page is a starting template. It must be reviewed by a real estate attorney licensed in each state where we operate, and by our accountant and professional liability insurer, before it is relied on.

Which pages this applies to

It applies to the whole site, and particularly to:

  • Residency and Domicile
  • Property Taxes and Homestead Exemptions
  • Financing Options
  • Florida, Texas and Arizona state guides
  • Market reports and everything under Insights
  • Every mortgage or monthly cost estimator on this site, each of which is clearly labelled as an estimate only

What a real estate brokerage may do

State real estate licensing law authorises us to do specific things and not others. Within our licensed scope, we can:

  • Represent you in the purchase or sale of real property in the states where we hold a licence.
  • Gather factual information from public records: parcel data, current assessed and taxable values, existing exemptions on a parcel, the taxing districts that levy against it, recorded documents and permit history.
  • Ask a county assessor or appraisal district to estimate what a bill would look like after a sale, and pass that estimate to you and to your accountant.
  • Explain in general terms how a mechanism works, such as what a homestead exemption is or what an assessment cap does.
  • Introduce you to accountants, attorneys, lenders and insurance agents.
  • Describe what we have seen in other transactions, clearly identified as anecdote rather than analysis.

What a real estate brokerage may not do

  • Advise you whether to change your domicile or residency, or tell you the tax consequence of doing so.
  • Calculate your tax position, prepare a return, or opine on how a statute applies to your circumstances.
  • Draft or advise on the legal effect of contracts, deeds, trusts, wills or entity structures. Interpreting the legal effect of a document is the practice of law.
  • Quote an insurance premium, advise on coverage adequacy, or bind cover.
  • Quote a mortgage rate, promise financing, or advise on which loan product suits you.
  • Advise on whether a property is a good investment, or forecast its value.

Where a question crosses one of those lines, we will say so and tell you which profession you need. That is not us being unhelpful. Giving advice we are not licensed to give would expose you to a wrong answer and expose us to a licensing complaint.

Specifically on tax and residency

State residency and domicile rules turn on facts, not on rules of thumb. Two households with identical properties and identical calendars can have different outcomes because of where their income is sourced, how their businesses are structured, where their families live, or what they filed in a prior year.

We will not tell you that a state is cheaper, that a purchase will reduce your tax, or that a particular number of days will produce a particular result. Anyone who tells you those things without reviewing your returns is guessing on your behalf.

See the residency and domicile guide for the framework, and take it to a professional.

Specifically on estimators and figures

The monthly cost estimators on property pages are arithmetic tools. They are not loan offers, rate quotes, pre-approvals, or predictions of what you will pay. They exclude items that matter, including utilities, maintenance, private mortgage insurance where applicable, closing costs and any special district assessment.

Illustrative figures across this site, including property tax rates, association fee ranges, insurance allowances, climate data, distances and market statistics, are placeholders or general indications. They must be verified against the county, the association, an insurance quote for the specific address, and the relevant primary source before you rely on them.

No relationship created

Reading this site, submitting a form or exchanging emails with us does not create a brokerage relationship, an agency relationship, an attorney-client relationship or any fiduciary duty. A brokerage relationship arises only under a signed written agreement.

Third-party information

Information sourced from public records, multiple listing services, associations, sellers and other third parties is deemed reliable but is not guaranteed. We do not independently verify every figure a third party supplies, and we tell you which figures we have verified and which we have not.
