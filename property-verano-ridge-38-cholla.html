About this house

A 2019 custom house on a 1.62-acre hillside lot at the northern edge of Verano Ridge, built by one of the community's approved builders on a plan drawn for this specific parcel. The whole house is on one level with zero-step entry at the front door, at the garage and at every exterior door onto the terrace. Interior doorways are 36 inches throughout, and the primary bathroom has a curbless shower with a fixed bench and a 42-inch entry.

The plan runs east to west along the contour, which is why it feels longer than its square footage suggests. Public rooms face south onto the pool terrace; bedrooms sit on the quieter north side looking up at the ridge. A detached casita of 640 square feet sits across the entry courtyard with its own bathroom, kitchenette and separate entrance.

The south wall of the great room pockets fully into the wall, which is a genuinely good detail in this climate for roughly seven months and a genuinely expensive one to operate in July. Deep overhangs and an exterior shade structure over the terrace do most of the work; the house is oriented and detailed for solar control rather than relying on glass coatings alone.

Mechanically it is in strong condition. Four-zone air conditioning installed 2019, a 14.2 kW owned solar array with a production history the seller will provide, two tankless water heaters, and a pool with both heating and chilling, the latter being the item people from cooler states do not expect to need and then use constantly in July and August.

What we would flag: the disturbance envelope is 0.71 acres of the 1.62-acre lot, so the apparent expansion room is smaller than the acreage implies, and any change requires design review approval. The exterior lighting must comply with the dark-sky ordinance, which limits what you can add. The driveway is steep enough at the top that a low vehicle will touch. And the well-established saguaros on the lot are protected under state law and county ordinance; moving or removing one requires a permit.

The neighbourhood

Cholla Rim Trail is a private lane of nine lots on the northern plat, the highest ground in the community at about 2,090 feet. That elevation gives a two to four degree overnight advantage over the gatehouse in summer and a clear north view across the state trust parcel that cannot be built on.

From this address: the gatehouse is 1.3 miles down a private road, the trailhead ramada 0.4 miles, the dog run 1.1 miles. Outside the gates, the nearest full grocery is 5.2 miles, a pharmacy 5.1 miles, the nearest restaurant cluster 5.5 miles, a hardware store 6.7 miles, and the nearest hospital with a twenty-four hour emergency department 8.3 miles. Phoenix Sky Harbor is 31 miles.

There is no sidewalk on the connecting road and no transit service within four miles. This is a car-dependent address and the drive to anything is measured in miles rather than minutes of walking. Within the community, the private trail network is used heavily for walking and running from October through April.

Verano Ridge is not an age-restricted community and makes no age-based occupancy claim. Its rules govern the property and its use, not who lives there.

Costs to plan for beyond the price

Association fee of $540 per month. County property tax, which will be reassessed on transfer and which depends on whether Arizona classifies the property as your primary residence. Homeowner insurance, which in this county is comparatively moderate because there is no coastal wind or flood exposure, though wildfire proximity to the preserve is a rating factor. Electricity, which for a 5,940 square foot house with a chilled pool runs several times higher in July than in January even with the solar array; ask for twelve months of records, not an average.

Then the desert-specific items: irrigation system monitoring for the native and low-water planting, which will die quickly in July if a line fails; pool service including the chiller; wildfire defensible-space maintenance the association inspects for; and pest control, because scorpion and rodent management in this setting is routine rather than exceptional.
