About this house

A 2011 estate home on the fourteenth fairway, on a 0.41-acre lot with a southern rear exposure. The plan is predominantly single level: the primary bedroom, two secondary bedrooms, the great room, kitchen and study are all on the ground floor with zero-step entry from the garage and from the front door. A fourth bedroom and a bathroom sit above the garage and are reached by a staircase, with no lift installed.

The great room opens through pocketing sliders to a screened lanai of roughly 640 square feet, with a heated pool, a covered outdoor kitchen and a gas fire feature. The rear exposure faces south across the fairway, which means sun on the pool terrace through the middle of the day for most of the year and direct afternoon light into the great room in winter.

The kitchen was rebuilt in 2021 with a ten-foot island, a gas cooktop on propane, two ovens and a walk-in pantry. The primary bathroom has a curbless shower with a 36-inch entry and a fixed bench. Interior doorways on the ground floor are 32 to 36 inches.

Mechanical condition is the reason we would put this house on a shortlist. The concrete tile roof was replaced in 2023, both air conditioning systems date from 2021 and 2022, the water heater is a 2022 heat-pump unit, and the glazing is impact-rated throughout rather than shutter-protected. In Southwest Florida those four items are the difference between a house you can leave for the summer and a house that will cost you a hundred thousand dollars in the first five years.

What we would flag: the pool cage screen is original and will need rescreening within a few years. The propane tank is leased rather than owned, and the lease transfers. The paver driveway has settled at the apron and needs relaying. And the fourth bedroom over the garage is genuinely separate from the rest of the house, which suits some households and does not suit others.

The neighbourhood

Heron Glen is a cul-de-sac of eleven homes on the eastern side of Aldermere, built between 2009 and 2012 by a single builder. It backs onto the fourteenth fairway of the north course with a preserve tract beyond, so the long view from the rear terrace will not change. The street has no through traffic.

From this address: the main gate is 0.8 miles, the clubhouse and dining room 1.1 miles, the fitness building and lap pool 0.9 miles, the tennis and pickleball courts 1.0 miles, and the dog park 1.4 miles. Outside the gates, the nearest full grocery is 2.9 miles, a pharmacy 2.7 miles, the nearest restaurant cluster 3.2 miles, and the nearest hospital with a twenty-four hour emergency department 8.9 miles. The nearest public library branch is 4.6 miles.

Golf carts are permitted on interior roads and on the boundary road to the shopping plaza. There is a sidewalk network within the community. The connecting arterial has no continuous pedestrian route, so trips outside the gates are made by car or cart.

Aldermere is not an age-restricted community and makes no age-based occupancy claim. Its rules govern the property and its use, not who lives there.

Costs to plan for beyond the price

Association fee of $1,180 per month. County property tax, which will be reassessed on transfer and will not match the current owner's bill. Homeowner insurance including wind, which for an impact-glazed 2011 house with a 2023 roof should be at the favourable end of the Collier County range, though you must get a bindable quote for this address rather than an estimate. Flood insurance if your lender requires it, which depends on the parcel's flood determination. Pool service, landscape maintenance beyond the association's common areas, pest control including termite bond, and a home-watch service for the months the house is empty. Club membership, if you want golf access, is a separate contract with its own initiation deposit and annual dues.
