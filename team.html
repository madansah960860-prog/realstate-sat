The people who will actually do the work

Meridian Shore Realty is a small brokerage. There is no lead-routing system here: the person you speak to in the consultation is the person who represents you through closing.

How the brokerage is structured

Meridian Shore Realty, LLC is a licensed real estate brokerage with one office, at 1420 Bayshore Boulevard, Suite 300, Sarasota, Florida 34236. We hold brokerage licences in Florida, Texas and Arizona. Every person listed on this page is either a licensed broker, a licensed agent of this brokerage, or unlicensed support staff who do not give real estate advice and are identified as such.

We are deliberately small. Six people can read association documents properly for the number of clients we take on. Sixty could not, without turning the reading into a checklist someone else completes.

How this business earns money

Stated plainly, in full, because a client should never have to work out how their broker is paid.

  • Commission on representation. Our main revenue. Agreed in writing with you before we tour anything on the buy side, or in the listing agreement on the sell side. Commission is negotiable and there is no standard rate. Who pays it is separately negotiable and is explained in full on the buying process page.
  • Referral fees from partner brokerages. When you are buying somewhere we are not licensed, we introduce a brokerage that is and receive a fee from their commission. It does not increase what you pay, and we tell you the arrangement and the percentage before you speak to them.
  • Fee-based concierge services. Relocation and downsizing coordination, billed as flat fees published on the service page. Optional, separate from commission, and you can decline them and still be represented.
  • Paid community placements. Developers and homeowner associations can pay to have a community featured on this site. Every paid placement is labelled as advertising wherever it appears. It does not change what our research says, and we have declined placements we could not write honestly about.
  • Partner referral relationships. Some moving, storage and estate-sale companies pay us a referral fee. We disclose that in the same conversation as the recommendation.
  • Free market reports and guides. No charge and no email gate. They exist because they generate consultation enquiries, which is how we get clients.

We do not accept referral fees from lenders, home inspectors, insurance agents, attorneys or accountants. We do not sell contact information. We do not accept payment to rank a property higher in search results on this site.

How we work with clients

One broker, named at the start, from consultation through closing. You will occasionally hear from Priya about research or scheduling and from Marguerite if you have engaged the concierge service, but the person responsible for your transaction does not change and does not hand you to an assistant.

We take on a limited number of buyer clients at a time. When we are at capacity we say so and offer either a waiting period or a referral to another brokerage, rather than accepting the work and doing it thinly.

We answer questions from people who are not clients and may never be. A large share of our week is spent on that, and it is the main reason the guides on this site exist.

What we will not do

  • Describe a community in terms of who lives there. Fair housing law prohibits it and we would not do it anyway. Fair Housing Notice.
  • Predict property values or describe a purchase as an investment opportunity.
  • Manufacture urgency. If competition is real we will show you the evidence; if it is not we will say so.
  • Give tax, legal or insurance advice. We hand our research to your professionals.
  • Take a referral fee from any professional whose independence you are relying on.
  • Claim to be the leading, top or number one anything, because we cannot substantiate it and neither can the brokerages that do.

Speak to one of us

Forty-five minutes with a licensed broker, by phone or video. No charge, no obligation, and no follow-up sequence unless you ask for one.
