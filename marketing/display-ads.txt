--- IMAGE AND PLACEMENT NOTES ---
1. Use photographs of property, architecture and landscape only. Exteriors at golden hour, interiors with
   natural light, coastline, golf landscape, palm-lined streets. Consistent 3:2 crops.
2. Do not use images of people. Imagery that signals a preferred demographic is a fair housing problem
   even when the ad copy is compliant.
3. No text overlay claims on images. No price, rate, fee or urgency graphics. No countdown creative.
4. Every image must be licensed for commercial use, with the licence on file before the campaign runs.

--- COMPLIANCE NOTES FOR WHOEVER RUNS THESE CAMPAIGNS ---
1. Self-identify every campaign as a Housing ad before it runs. This disables age, gender, parental
   status, marital status and ZIP code targeting and limits audience targeting.
2. Complete Google advertiser identity verification. The verified business identity must match the legal
   brokerage name, address, phone and email published on the website exactly.
3. Exclude placements that would associate housing ads with demographic targeting by proxy.
4. Landing page relevance: whatever an ad promises must appear prominently on the page it lands on.
5. Every listing, price, licence number and testimonial on the destination site is currently placeholder
   content. Do not run paid traffic to it until that content is replaced and verified.
6. Have a real estate attorney and a fair housing reviewer sign off on this copy before it runs.
